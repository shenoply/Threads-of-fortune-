THREADS OF FORTUNE - STANDOFF SCREEN ART (30 prompts)
The screen when your caravan meets bandits, like Bannerlord: their leader in front with his men behind him on the right; you in front with your men behind you on the left.

HOW THE SCREEN IS BUILT (so it always matches your real party)
- A backdrop of the place where you are (10, one per battlefield type).
- YOU: the game uses your wardrobe figure, dressed exactly as you are dressed. No new art needed.
- Behind you: one figure per troop type you have hired, repeated in rows (up to 6 shown, then "x12").
  If you have no men, you stand alone.
- The bandits: their leader in front, his men repeated behind him, as many as the band really has.
- Back rows are drawn smaller and darker, so it reads as a crowd with depth.

RULES FOR CHATGPT
- Backdrops: attach stall-seller.jpg for the painting style. Empty ground, no people.
- Figures: make #11 (guard) FIRST. When you like it, attach it to every other figure prompt and add at the top:
  "Match the attached figure exactly: same painting style, same light from the upper left, same scale."
- YOUR men face RIGHT (towards the bandits). BANDITS face LEFT (towards you). Do not mix this up.
- Transparent background, or plain flat black. I cut them out here.
- One image per message. Save each with the file name shown.
- Figures go in public/art/standoff/units/, backdrops in public/art/standoff/.

A wide painted backdrop for the moment two armed parties meet on the road in a game set in 1925.

Scene: a flat gravel desert with a dusty caravan road running away to the horizon, a ruined mud-brick wall far off on the left, thorn bushes.
View: from standing eye height, looking across the ground, like a stage set. The horizon sits at about 40% from the top.
The whole lower half is open, flat, walkable ground running from the left edge to the right edge: figures will be placed standing on it later (my party on the left, the other party on the right).

Empty scene: NO people, NO animals, NO camels, NO horses, NO carts, NO text.
Lighting: late afternoon sun from the upper left, long shadows, a little dust in the air.
Style: Orientalist oil painting in the manner of Jean-Léon Gérôme, warm golden late-afternoon light from the upper left, rich but muted colours, visible brushwork, the same painting style as the attached image.
Canvas: landscape 1536x1024.

Pose: the whole horse and rider seen from the side, three-quarter view, the animal's head pointing to the LEFT edge. Ready to fight, eyes on his leader.
Lit from the upper left like the rest of the set.
The animal and rider fill about 90% of the canvas width, feet/hooves at the bottom edge area. Centred.

Only this one figure. No ground, no shadow on the ground, no background, no border, no text.
Background: fully transparent (PNG with alpha). If that is not possible, plain flat black.
Style: Orientalist oil painting in the manner of Jean-Léon Gérôme, warm golden late-afternoon light from the upper left, rich but muted colours, visible brushwork, the same painting style as the attached image.
Canvas: landscape 1536x1024.
