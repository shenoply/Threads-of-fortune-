THREADS OF FORTUNE - THE SHOP CATALOGUE BOOK (prompts for ChatGPT)
====================================================================

WHAT IT IS
A printed 1920s trade catalogue, like the mail-order catalogues in Red Dead Redemption 2, but for Cairo:
"THE LEVANT MERCHANT'S CATALOGUE". Every shop (antique dealer, chemist, tailor, general store, gunsmith)
is a section with its own coloured ribbon tab. Each page shows items as black-ink engravings in frames,
with the name, a short line of description and the price underneath. You tap an item to buy it.

HOW WE SPLIT THE WORK
- ChatGPT paints the BOOK (cover, empty pages, tabs, shop headers) and the ITEM ENGRAVINGS.
- ALL TEXT is left EMPTY in the pictures. I write names, descriptions and prices in the game in a
  period typeface, so prices can change and nothing is misspelled.
- Tailor and gunsmith items use the wardrobe pictures we already have, turned into engravings in code,
  so they need no new art.

STYLE LINE (put at the end of EVERY prompt):
"Style: authentic 1920s printed trade catalogue, black-ink steel engraving with fine cross-hatching,
printed on aged cream paper with slight foxing, no colour except where stated. No text, no letters,
no numbers anywhere."

Each page has the SAME empty layout:

Five coloured ribbon tabs (dark red, ochre, blue, green, black) stick out of the right page's edge,
matching the cover.
