HASSAN — VOICE LINES, PART 3: YOUR OWN CARPETS
=============================================

Same as before: the phone's own recorder app, a quiet room, 15–20 cm from your mouth, one take for the
whole section, 1–2 seconds' pause between lines. Flub one? Say "take two" and read it again.
Start by saying "G". Send me the file and I'll cut it into the game.

How to say them: you are proud of these carpets. They are yours, not stock. Warm, a little boastful,
never shouting.

G  AT THE WEAVERY AND OVER YOUR OWN CARPETS
-------------------------------------------
Handing the weaver your drawing
G1. Weave it exactly as I drew it. Not one knot of your own.
G2. This one is mine, from the first thread. Take care of it.
G3. My father would have laughed at this drawing. Then he would have bought it.

Collecting it off the loom
G4. There it is. My own carpet. Nobody else in Cairo has this one.
G5. Unroll it slowly. I want to see it come off the loom.
G6. Every knot as I drew it. Good work, my friend.

Hanging it framed at the stall
G7. This one is not for sale. It stays on my wall.
G8. Let them look. Let them ask who made it.

When a buyer looks up at it
G9. You like it? I drew it myself. It is not for sale.
G10. That one? My own design. Perhaps I will weave you another.

Selling one of your own
G11. You are buying a piece of me. Look after it.
G12. My own design. You will not find another like it.
