THREADS OF FORTUNE: FAMILY AND AGING BATCH (22 images)

The hero ages as the years pass (he is 45 in March 1925). He can marry and have a son and a
daughter who grow up and later help in the business. These pictures show them at each age.

HOW TO USE
1. Open ChatGPT (image generation). Use a NEW chat for each group (A, B, C, D) so the faces stay
   consistent inside a group.
2. Attach the reference images named for that group (links at the bottom), paste the prompt, send.
   Do the images of a group one after another in the same chat.
3. Save each result under the name shown (A1-hero-stall-50.png ...).
4. Put all 22 in the Drive folder "Threads of Fortune – Family and aging batch" and tell Claude.

VERY IMPORTANT FOR GROUP A (THE HERO)
The hero's clothes are separate layers drawn on top of these pictures. So every aged version must
keep EXACTLY the same pose, outline, size and position as the attached base picture, and the same
plain cream sleeveless undershirt. Only his face, beard, skin and hands grow older. Transparent
background, same canvas size.

Shared style for every image: hand-painted, semi-realistic, warm light, the same painting style as
the attached references, 1920s-1940s Egypt, no text, no border, no watermark.

Attach: ref-P1-portrait-style.jpg (style and framing only; NOT her face)
======================================================================
Nour is the daughter of a respected Cairo cloth merchant from the Khan el-Khalili, clever with
numbers, calm and proud. Chest-up portrait, three-quarter view, the same framing and painting style
as the attached portrait, warm interior light, a softly painted mashrabiya window behind her.

B1-nour-28.png
Nour at 28 (1925): an Egyptian woman with dark eyes, a strong straight nose and a composed half smile, wearing a black silk tarha head covering loosely over dark hair, a small gold crescent pendant, a dark green dress with a high neck. Square 1024x1024. No text.

B2-nour-38.png
The same woman ten years later, at 38 (1935): same face, a few fine lines, a more assured look, a dark blue dress in the 1930s Cairo style, the same tarha, a gold bangle. Same framing and background. Square 1024x1024.

B3-nour-48.png
The same woman at 48 (1945): same face, some grey at the temples under the tarha, lines at the eyes, the calm authority of the mistress of a prosperous house, a plum-coloured dress, reading glasses held in one hand. Same framing and background. Square 1024x1024.
