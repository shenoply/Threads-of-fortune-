ARRAN LAB: the smallest extra art batch (2 images)

Reference for style and light: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/shenoply/Threads-of-fortune-/main/art-src/arran/13-lab-room.png
Style for both: the same painted, warm, late-afternoon realism as 13-lab-room. Giza, 1925. No modern
equipment, no plastic, no electronics, no people. Absolutely no text, letters, numbers, labels or watermarks.

L1  lab-fibres.png   (needed)
Size: 1536 x 512 (3:1), opaque, dark background.
Where it goes: the result card for a fibre test, full width of the lower panel, above the evidence.
Prompt:
"Three round microscope fields of view side by side on a near-black background, evenly spaced,
each a perfect circle about 440 px across with a soft dark vignette at the edge, as seen through a
1920s brass monocular microscope at about 200x, transmitted warm lamplight. LEFT circle: three or
four sheep's wool fibres, cream and madder-red, each showing clear overlapping cuticle scales like
roof tiles. MIDDLE circle: cotton fibres, pale, flat twisted ribbons with a visible lumen, like
twisted flattened tubes. RIGHT circle: silk filaments, smooth glassy rods with no scales and no
twist, a few dyed deep indigo, with a soft sheen. Scientific illustration painted in a realistic
period style, sharp focus in the centre of each circle. No text, no labels, no numbers, no scale bar."
No-text zones: the whole image (labels are added by the game in HTML under each circle).
