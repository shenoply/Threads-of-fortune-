THREADS OF FORTUNE - THE NEW STALL (prompts for ChatGPT)
=========================================================

HOW THIS WORKS (read once)
- Everything is built on ONE empty stall picture (#1). No people, no cat, empty shelves.
- Weather and time of day (#2-#10) are EDITS of #1: same stall, only the light and the sky change.
- Every prop (radio, coffee table, antiques) and every cat spot is an EDIT of #1 that adds that one
  thing and changes nothing else. I cut each one out by comparing with #1, so it lines up exactly
  and can be switched on or off in the game (buy an antique -> it appears on its shelf).
- The hero stands on the LEFT, buyers stand on the RIGHT, the rug is unrolled on the counter in the
  CENTRE-BOTTOM. Those three areas are kept clear, so nothing ever clashes with the seller or buyer art.
- Always use the SAME chat, attach #1 for every edit, and download at full size.

FILE NAMES: save as public/art/stall2/<name> using the name in [brackets].

#1  THE EMPTY STALL   [stall-base.jpg]
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Painterly realistic illustration, 1920s Giza bazaar, the same warm painted style as the attached
reference (attach stall-seller.jpg). Landscape 1536x1024. Eye-level view from the lane, looking
straight into a small rug merchant's stall under a striped canvas awning on wooden poles.
No people and no animals anywhere in the stall.
Left third: the stall's side wall with hanging rugs high up, plain rug-covered floor below; keep
this area uncluttered from knee to head height because a standing man will be placed there.
Centre back: a whitewashed mud-brick back wall with THREE EMPTY wooden shelves, one above the other,
between 30% and 68% across and 12% to 55% down. The shelves must be completely empty.
Centre, in front of the shelves: a low round brass tray table on a folding wooden stand, with one
red floor cushion beside it; the tray is EMPTY. Place it between 34% and 48% across, 48% to 62% down.
Centre-bottom foreground: a wide low wooden counter covered in a plain dark cloth, from 32% to 68%
across and 62% to the bottom; its top surface is EMPTY (rugs will be unrolled on it).
Right third: the stall opens onto the dusty lane; behind it the busy bazaar in soft focus, palm
trees and the two pyramids against the sky. Keep the lane in the right third open and empty of
nearby people (distant blurred crowd only), because a customer will be placed there.
Top: the awning's front beam with two empty iron lantern hooks. Soft clear midday light,
gentle shadows under the awning. No text, no signs with letters, no border.
