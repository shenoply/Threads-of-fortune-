THREADS OF FORTUNE - REDRAW THE 15 BUYERS FOR THE NEW STALL
=============================================================

WHY: the current buyer pictures are cut off at both sides and have their own table painted in.
The new pictures show each person whole from the waist up, with nothing cut off, so they fit
the new stall cleanly.

HOW
- One buyer per message. Attach that buyer's current picture: art/portraits/<id>-stall.png
  (for example antonios-stall.png). If you don't have it, attach art/portraits/<id>.jpg instead.
- Paste the MAIN PROMPT below, then that buyer's line from the list.
- Do Father Antonios first and send him to me. I'll test him on the stall before you do the other 14.
- Download each result at full size (tap Download; don't send a screenshot).
- Name the file <id>-stall2.png, e.g. antonios-stall2.png.


MAIN PROMPT (paste this every time)
-----------------------------------
Redraw the person in the attached image as the SAME person: same face, age, hair, beard,
clothes, colours, accessories and warm painted style. Only the framing and the pose change.

- Portrait image, 1024 x 1536.
- Show them from the waist up, standing behind a counter that you do NOT draw: both forearms
  and hands rest flat or relaxed on an invisible counter top at 80% of the way down the image.
  Below that line, just continue their clothes to the bottom of the image.
- The WHOLE person must be inside the picture: both shoulders, both arms, both elbows and both
  hands clearly inside, with empty space between them and the left and right edges.
  Nothing touches or is cut by the sides.
- The top of the head (or hat) sits about 12% down from the top edge.
- Body turned slightly to the LEFT of the image, face looking toward the left, as if talking to
  a merchant standing on the left.
- Warm golden late-afternoon light coming from the upper left.
- Background: plain flat pure white, nothing else. No table, no rug, no counter, no props on
  the counter, no floor, no shadow on the background, no text.

IF IT GOES WRONG
- Cut off at the side: "The person is cut off at the edge. Zoom out so both arms and hands are
  fully inside the picture with white space on both sides."
- Table or rug drawn: "Remove the table and rug completely. Plain white background only; the
  hands rest on an invisible counter."
- Face changed: "Keep the face exactly as in the attached image."
